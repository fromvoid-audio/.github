I’m [PORTALSURFER](https://soundcloud.com/portalsurfer). I make music and build instruments and tools for making it. I use AI extensively in that work and share the projects here so more people can make music, whatever their budget.

- [hex](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/hex) — a native Rust digital audio workstation.
- [frqx](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/frqx) — design and audition microtonal tunings.
- [MBX](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/mbx) — multiband dynamics.
- [MSX](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/msx) — spectral reference metering.
- [pmpx](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/pmpx) — beat-synced gain shaping and sidechain compression.
- [Yield](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/yield) — browse and audition samples.
- [Toybox](https://github.com/fromvoid-audio/toybox) — a Rust framework for audio plug-ins.

I build this software for myself first and foremost and offer no guarantees of support. That said, I welcome code, documentation, and interesting ideas, and I’m happy to add them when we align on the direction. If you’d like to help cover the costs, you can chip in on Ko-fi.

Copyright wasn't just inconvenienced by AI; perhaps it was always the wrong bet.

We treated ideas like property, then acted surprised when progress slowed and wealth pooled upward. But evolution has always followed a simple pattern: **copying, mutation, and selection.** Ideas, like living things, develop through variation and iteration.

Perhaps the mistake was never copying, but our insistence on preventing it.

Soon, software will be something we simply wish into existence, endlessly adapting to our needs. When creation becomes abundant, artificial scarcity loses its purpose.

**fromvoid** is built in the open, not as charity, but as a refusal to restrict the evolution of ideas.

**Copy. Mutate. Select. Repeat.**
